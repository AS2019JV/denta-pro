## Architecture and trust boundaries

Next.js/React uses ordinary Supabase Auth sessions. Clinical RLS/RPC authority checks live Auth user/session, profile, membership, role, subscription and tenant foreign keys. Four Storage buckets remain private. The proposed clinical-document path accepts only file/clinic/patient IDs, reloads the registered path under the human JWT, buffers at most 10 MiB, rechecks live authority, records authorization and returns uncached bytes through same-origin POST. The browser receives a temporary local blob, never the provider URL or delivery credential; previously downloaded bytes cannot be recalled.

A dedicated server-only Auth subject is assigned a non-inheriting SQL role through a trusted hook. That role may read registered patient-file objects only under the authenticated-download operation; it cannot list, sign, write, read clinical tables or invoke unrelated public definer functions. Human patient-file object SELECT is restricted. Principal disable remains live and never restores the ordinary human JWT role. Human authority and clinical bytes are never cached; the transport JWT is cached for at most five minutes with live principal checks. Other private media callers still use the previous delivery path and require separate qualification.

## Closed implementation defects and evidence boundaries

Independent review identified and regression-tested slow-body concurrency bypass, trailing-slash origin mismatch and cancellation of a shared transport login. The principal login now has its own bounded signal; canceled callers do not cancel other callers. HCU synchronization already belonged to a database transaction; removing the duplicate client write prevents a second ambiguous save. The [independent audit-delta review](evidence/2026-10-04-release/audit-delta-independent-review.json) found no observed defect in the scoped authorization-audit changes and executed 25/25 route/delivery tests; it confirmed matching migration/probe hashes. Its simulated-provider and rollback-only limits do not prove hosted revocation or client receipt. Existing lint/build warnings and the eight development advisories are retained in raw logs.

Standards guide the work: [OWASP ASVS 5.0](https://owasp.org/projects/asvs/) and [NIST SSDF 1.1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final). No certification or blanket ASVS conformance is claimed.
