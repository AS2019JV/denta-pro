## Reproduced blocker and false-positive review

**P1: cached private-object download outlives authorization.** The same unexpired doctor JWT and known synthetic PDF path yield null role, clinical42501, empty list and sign400/NoSuchKey after committed removal. Both canonical and `/object/authenticated/` downloads return200, `cf-cache-status:HIT`, and the original70-byte SHA. Anonymous and fresh nonce requests deny. Actual global logout and temporary Auth ban reproduce fresh-authority denial plus cached200; ban lifted afterward. This is stale authorized edge content, not anonymous public access or a failure of fresh RLS. Persistence across other regions and final cache duration have not been measured.

Earlier `prove-auth-revoked.json` fails before logout because its positive control requests another doctor's private avatar. It is not Auth revocation evidence. The separate `auth-revocation-1791068059581.json` uses an authorized patient-file control and actually executes logout/ban. Prior transport/RPC-shape failures are preserved and not counted as vulnerabilities.

Supabase documents [private per-user CDN caching](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/cdn/fundamentals) and [token expiry/cache TTL independence](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/cdn/smart-cdn). An endpoint switch, request no-cache header, client nonce or shorter signed URL cannot prove denial of direct cached requests. Do not delete clinical documents for ordinary revocation or open buckets. Resolve provider containment or review a server-mediated object architecture, then re-test positive warm → revoke → same-URL denial for every bucket and session/profile/membership state.

A new synthetic file uploaded with explicit `private, no-store, max-age=0, must-revalidate` still received CDN HIT on repeated reads and200 during the removal experiment: [positive header/byte control](evidence/2026-10-03-hosted-api/attempt-1/nostore-prepare-1791069160252.json), [retained-JWT replay](evidence/2026-10-03-hosted-api/attempt-1/nostore-revoked-1791069187350.json). That replay artifact lacks its own contemporaneous role/SQL/sign negative controls and is only header/cache corroboration; it does not independently prove revocation. The stronger separately linked removal/logout/ban artifacts establish the P1. Response prepends `public`; no app patch claiming header-only remediation was shipped. Membership restored afterward. The named two-second signed-URL matrix probes did warm then expire successfully in their observed environment; they are retained as direct evidence, while general CDN/TTL guarantees remain unproven.

Independent adversarial review challenged cache scope, false positives, CI binding and evidence completeness. Future Storage probe success now requires anonymous denial explicitly and rejects InvalidJWT/transport errors as authorization evidence. Future no-store replay requires contemporaneous role null, clinical42501 and sign NoSuchKey before downloads. Three regression tests exercise the probe classification failures; these helper changes do not retroactively upgrade recorded hosted outcomes. Original matrix JWTs are preserved, not refreshed to conceal expiry.
